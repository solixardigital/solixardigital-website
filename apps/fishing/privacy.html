Privacy Policy

Fishing

Effective Date: February 17, 2026

Solixar Digital LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Fishing mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Phone number (used for authentication via SMS verification)
  • Display name
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • Bio (optional)
  • Match preferences (gender and age range)

Camera

With your permission, we access your device camera to allow you to take a profile photo. Photos are resized and compressed before upload. We do not access your camera for any other purpose.

Contacts

With your permission, we access your device contacts to enable our mutual friend matching feature. Your raw contact data never leaves your device. Phone numbers from your contacts are converted into one-way cryptographic hashes (SHA-256) on your device before being uploaded. These hashes cannot be reversed to recover the original phone numbers. We use these hashes solely to identify mutual connections between users.

User Content

We store content you create in the App, including:

  • Profile information and photos
  • Match preferences
  • Hashed contact data (for matching purposes)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Authenticate your identity via SMS verification
  • Display your profile to potential matches
  • Find second-degree connections (friends of friends) using hashed contact data
  • Filter matches based on your stated preferences
  • Improve the App and fix bugs

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using:

  • Supabase - Cloud database and authentication (hosted in the USA)

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Contact phone numbers are hashed with SHA-256 before leaving your device and cannot be reversed.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase - Authentication and data storage (Privacy Policy)

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in these circumstances:

  • With other users - Your display name, profile photo, and phone number are shown to users who match with you through mutual contacts
  • Service providers - With third-party services listed above, solely to operate the App
  • Legal requirements - If required by law or to protect our rights

Your Data Rights

Accessing Your Data

You can:

  • View your data - Access all your profile information within the App
  • Update your data - Edit your profile and preferences at any time
  • Revoke permissions - Disable camera or contacts access in your device settings

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account and all associated data by emailing support@solixardigital.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and including:

  • The phone number associated with your account
  • Confirmation that you want to delete your account

We will process your request within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your profile and account information
  • Your profile photo
  • All hashed contact data you uploaded
  • Match preferences
  • Push notification tokens
  • Any other data associated with your account

This action cannot be undone.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. After account deletion, we retain no personal data.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy in the App or sending you a notification.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 29, 2026

Solixar Digital LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates Project Unicorn (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password. We may also collect your display name, gender, and pronouns if you choose to provide them during onboarding.

Chat Messages

The App is an AI companion chatbot. We store your chat messages with your AI companion(s) to provide conversation continuity and the companion memory feature. Messages are associated with your account and your specific companion.

Companion Data

We store the personality configuration, preferences, and memory data for each AI companion you create. This includes personality traits, communication style, backstory, voice guide, and example messages that define your companion's behavior.

Companion Avatar Images

If you generate an AI avatar or upload a photo for your companion, the image is stored in our cloud storage associated with your companion. AI-generated avatars are created using third-party image generation services.

Photos and Media

The App may request access to your device's photo library solely for the purpose of uploading a companion avatar image. We do not access, scan, or collect any other photos from your device. Photo library access is entirely optional and only initiated by you.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's core functionality (AI companion chat)
  • To remember your companion's personality and your conversation history
  • To store and display companion avatar images
  • To authenticate your account and maintain your session
  • To improve the App's features and user experience

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to provide the App's functionality:

  • Anthropic (Claude API) - Powers the AI companion's conversational abilities. Your chat messages are sent to Anthropic's servers for processing. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Gemini API) - Powers AI avatar image generation. Companion appearance descriptions are sent to Google's servers. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase - Provides our backend infrastructure including authentication, database, and file storage. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo / EAS - Used for app building and distribution. See Expo's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase cloud servers with row-level security policies ensuring that only you can access your own data. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and authentication tokens to protect your account.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual chat histories through the App's settings. You can delete your entire account and all associated data (companions, messages, memories, and images) through the App's settings, which permanently removes all your data from our servers.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data through the App (chat history, memories, companion settings)
  • Delete your chat history for any companion
  • Delete individual companions and all their associated data
  • Delete your entire account and all data
  • Request a copy of your data by contacting us

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 18, we will take steps to delete that information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. We only share data with the third-party services listed above as necessary to provide the App's functionality.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date.
